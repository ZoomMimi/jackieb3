What a day on the water! Mike and I were so happy to have our friends Scott and Tracy Wall join us for the run up to St. Augustine. Scott is a West Point classmate of Mike's, and having them aboard the Jackie B III for this stretch of the ICW made the miles just fly by. We left around 10:30 in the morning with a light ESE breeze and warm temperatures — honestly just about perfect boating weather.

The run up was only about 23 nautical miles, but there was so much to see along the way. As we approached St. Augustine, the landmarks just kept coming. First we spotted the old fort — that ancient coquina stone structure standing guard right at the waterfront, just as it has for hundreds of years. Then the gorgeous skyline of the old city came into view, with all those red-roofed towers and steeples, and the Bridge of Lions stretching across the water. I never get tired of coming into St. Augustine from the water. It really is one of the prettiest arrivals on the whole Loop.

We even passed the *Capt Trey*, a working shrimp boat heading out with her nets spread wide — a good reminder that this is still a working waterfront, not just a pretty postcard.

Once we were tied up and settled, the marina card told us everything we needed to know — gate code, bathroom code, wifi password — the essentials of life afloat! We got ourselves sorted out and then headed to the Conch House for dinner. What a great spot. The four of us sat outside and soaked it all in. Good food, good friends, and St. Augustine doing what it does best.

Such a fun day. So glad Scott and Tracy made the trip with us!
