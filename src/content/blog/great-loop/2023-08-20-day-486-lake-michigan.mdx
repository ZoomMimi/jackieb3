We didn't go far today — just 8.8 nautical miles and barely an hour underway — but sometimes the short days turn into the best ones.

We left Kalamazoo Lake in the morning with calm conditions, a light southwest breeze, and comfortable temperatures. As we came into Holland Harbor I kept getting distracted by what was happening in the sky above the shoreline. There were paragliders — motorized ones, it looked like — just drifting along above the bluff and the beach, completely unhurried, while we puttered in below them on the water. I took probably too many photos trying to capture them, and as you can see from the results I am not exactly a wildlife photographer. They were tiny little specks against that gorgeous blue sky, but I could not stop watching them. What a way to see the Lake Michigan shoreline.

We pulled into Eldean Shipyard and got settled for the day. I will say that short travel days have their advantages — mainly that you arrive early enough to actually *do* something.

And do something we did. That evening we ended up out with a fun group of people and I'm afraid the photos speak for themselves. Mike and I are, shall we say, looking very relaxed in our selfie. The group shot is a little sideways — or maybe that was just how the evening was going by that point. Either way, everyone was happy and laughing and that's really all you need to know.

I should add — we are working our way back up through the Great Lakes toward Canada. We made this same run through the North Channel last year and loved it so much we are doing it again. Coming back to these waters already feels like returning to somewhere familiar and wonderful. More on that soon.
