We had a land day today — no engines, no locks, just good old-fashioned terra firma. And honestly? Sometimes that's exactly what the doctor ordered.

The highlight of the afternoon was axe throwing. I'll be honest — I was not at all sure I was going to be any good at this. But I gave it my best shot (pun fully intended), and I have to say there is something deeply satisfying about hurling an axe across a room. The scoring screen on the wall tracked every throw, which was either encouraging or humbling depending on the moment. I'll let you draw your own conclusions from the photo.

We also made a quick stop — I'm not entirely sure what Mike was hoping to accomplish at the check-in kiosk, but I spotted his American Airlines AAdvantage account on the tablet and noticed the loyalty points situation was, shall we say, a work in progress. Five hundred award miles and zero loyalty points. We are clearly not prioritizing air travel this year. The Jackie B III is our airline.

The drive back gave us a little gift — a beautiful rainbow stretched right over the highway. After all the miles we've put in on this Loop, I never get tired of a good rainbow. It felt like a proper send-off for the day.

Back to Canada and the North Channel soon — it's hard to believe we're getting to do that stretch again. The first time through was so spectacular that I wasn't sure anything could top it. I may have to eat those words.

"He set his rainbow in the cloud, and it shall be a sign of the covenant between me and the earth."
