We had been looking forward to a proper do-nothing day, and Holland, Michigan delivered in the best possible way. We pulled our beach chairs right down to the water's edge — and I do mean *in* the water — and just sat there while the little waves rolled over our feet. Honestly, it was glorious. The water was cool and clear, and the sand was soft, and we didn't apologize to anyone for staying exactly there for a very long time.

![](photo1)

That first photo is just blue sky — brilliant, deep, almost unreal blue — and I think it perfectly captures what the whole day felt like. Wide open and peaceful.

![](photo2)

Mike and I had our best smiles going for the selfie, hats on, sunglasses doing their job. I'll be honest, my hat does most of the heavy lifting when it comes to sun protection. Mike on the other hand just goes shirtless and seems completely unbothered.

![](photo3)

There is something deeply satisfying about a feet-in-the-water photo. We took several, because apparently that's who we are now. The waves would wash up, we'd wiggle our toes, and life felt very simple and very good.

![](photo4)

I had my cold drink, my beach chair half-submerged in the shallows, my phone safely zipped into its waterproof case around my neck — priorities — and I was perfectly content. We are back in Canada waters for this stretch of our journey, and the Great Lakes continue to surprise us with how genuinely beautiful they are. The North Channel has been breathtaking, but an afternoon like this one reminds you that sometimes the best thing a place can offer is just a quiet beach and a reason to sit still.

"He leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul."
Psalms 23:2-3 NIV
