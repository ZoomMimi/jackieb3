We are back in the Chicago area for a bit before we head north toward Canada again — and what a wonderful reason to pause the loop life for a day! We had the joy of celebrating a very special little girl's first birthday, and I have to say, this baby girl is absolutely precious.

The party started out at the backyard play set, where the birthday girl got her first real swing experience in that little bucket seat. She was barefoot and adorable in her red floral outfit, clutching a little yellow wiffle ball like it was the greatest treasure on earth. I could have watched her swing all afternoon — that face!

Then came the cake smash, which she approached with the kind of serious focus that tells you she already has excellent priorities in life. Frosting everywhere — face, hands, tray — and not one single regret. The colorful balloons tied to her high chair (pink, lavender, yellow, and teal) made the whole scene look like a little celebration cloud had landed right in the backyard.

Mike did a great job working the rainbow piñata — he had it on a rope from the tree and was doing his best to make it just difficult enough to be fun. The kids took turns swinging away with a foam pool noodle, which I thought was a very sensible choice given the ages involved. When that rainbow finally burst, every single child hit the grass at the same time. It was chaos, it was joyful, and it was exactly what a birthday party should be.

What a gift it is to step off the boat for a day and just be present for moments like these. We'll get back to the water soon enough — Canada is calling — but today belonged to this little birthday girl.

"Children are a heritage from the Lord, offspring a reward from him."
