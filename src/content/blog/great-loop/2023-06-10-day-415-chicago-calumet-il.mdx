What a wonderful reason to be off the boat for a day! Our little Eliza turned one, and we celebrated in the backyard with all the trimmings — a baby swing with yellow chains, pink and purple balloons, a rainbow piñata, and of course, birthday cake.

We are in the middle of our return trip to Canada — the North Channel is calling us back — but there was absolutely no question we were going to be here for this milestone. Some things are more important than making miles.

The day started with Eliza in that little green bucket swing, bare feet dangling, clutching a bright yellow wiffle ball like it was the greatest treasure she had ever found. She looked so pleased with herself. I could have watched her all afternoon.

Then came the cake. Oh my goodness. She went after it with such enthusiasm — frosting from cheek to cheek, eyes wide, hand diving back in for more. That expression on her face was priceless. Mike was right there for all of it, and I have a feeling this is one birthday none of us will forget.

Later the older kids had their turn with a rainbow piñata that Mike was working overtime to keep in the air. I think he got quite the workout yanking that rope up and down while they took their swings! They finally broke it open and dove in to collect every last piece of candy. Typical. It was loud and chaotic and absolutely wonderful.

What a gift it is to be grandparents. Happy first birthday, sweet Eliza. You are so very loved.

"Children are a heritage from the Lord, offspring a reward from him."
