There was a lot more to do in Key Largo beyond diving the reefs. We stayed at Marina Del Mar and had three pools with hot tubs to choose from. There was pickleball nearby that we took advantage of. Mike of course found a sand bar to Kite on. I didn't tag a long. Stayed at the pools with shade and not so gusty. This photo is of him returning from the sand bar.

![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiVaDc2TnYb9dHSVCSHw05uTG7IPZmB54PH2ERz3AaNq38_PCdGKcvd4fbmDsaUjLULPnJInajffxNccmcXB1u03Jw76JmcmPsCBatUz1sc36Yzf9rGU_bv0kCmXSaLCg5dPU9F1uQxXFBxwkHsKL-5ig1eHzS5wUuo0PMuBOVrSgepRgVxumlLRi4bAqo/w558-h419/IMG_7887.jpeg)

On a calm day, Mike led a group of us to the sandbar where he kites. We had a blast standing in the warm water looking for Eagle rays and turtles. We even saw a nurse shark.

![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEi2me4jmSpw6OAayCSLsQhbKLlSSpeRTZGxHrRH2N-PWo3D1w6ileF1e_D5YY9UvHLJwrEJJlxHE0YXCrqmvksUhtSr_-oUPuqFu6sJbrmhlh6HOfE2tqtv908f2o5A-nKBzYn-O_Oidb2oiWvS786n58e7qp2qO4wee8YxRkovfOhOAiaUd9IhUGueux0/w552-h414/IMG_6551.jpeg)

The original African Queen from the Humphrey Bogart and Katherine Hepburn is berthed in the canal at the resort we are staying at. Many people take an excursion on her. So she went past our boat daily.
