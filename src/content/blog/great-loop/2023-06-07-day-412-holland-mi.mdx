It's funny how a boating trip can turn into a very landlocked kind of day sometimes. We're back in the North Channel area of Ontario for this summer's return to Canada — more on that soon — but today found us in Holland, Michigan, doing what any sensible boater does when they have a little downtime: wandering around on shore and poking through someone else's stuff at a garage sale.

The highlight of the morning, without a doubt, was spotting a porcupine up in the trees. I have to be honest — my photos are absolutely terrible. I kept zooming in and the poor thing just got blurrier and blurrier, but I promise you there is a porcupine in those pictures somewhere. You might need to use your imagination a little. Mike could see it just fine, which means I spent a good five minutes pointing my phone at what I was pretty sure was the right branch. The lush green canopy up here in Michigan is gorgeous this time of year, and apparently it is also an excellent hiding place for wildlife that does not want to be photographed.

We also came across a wooden staircase tucked into the woods — the kind that makes you feel like you've stumbled onto a park trail you weren't expecting. Very charming.

The garage sale was a bonus. Someone had a pull-up and dip station out on their patio for a very reasonable price, and there was a portable air conditioner priced at $50 — though a closer look showed it had seen better days and was missing some pieces. Mike examined it thoroughly, as he does with everything, and we decided to pass on that one. Can't say I was too heartbroken about not hauling a broken air conditioner anywhere.

All in all, a quieter day, but sometimes those are the best ones.
