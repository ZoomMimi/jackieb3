We have been so looking forward to getting back into Canadian waters this summer, but first we had a few more miles of Lake Michigan to cover. Today we departed Harbor View Marina and made our way north toward Frankfort, Michigan — a relatively short run of about 6 nautical miles, though we were underway for nearly six hours all told, which tells you something about how we like to take our time and enjoy the scenery.

The lake was in a cooperative mood as we headed out. That Garmin screen was showing 386 feet of water beneath us — Lake Michigan does not mess around when it comes to depth! — and the water temperature was reading right around 60 degrees. Chilly enough to remind you this is not the Gulf of Mexico.

Along the way we passed a beautiful white lighthouse standing tall at the end of a long pier. It's such a striking structure — all white with that distinctive boxy shape and the porthole windows — and I could see a few brave souls out walking the pier right up beside it.

As we came into Frankfort and turned into Betsie Lake, the fog had started rolling in and we spotted another lighthouse peeking through the haze on shore. It had that classic look — dark upper tower on a white base, kind of ghostly and beautiful all at once in the mist. We made our way through the inlet and got ourselves tied up for the evening.

And then came my favorite part of any docking night — looking back at the Jackie B III lit up from below. We have underwater blue LED lights on the swim platform, and I have to say they never get old. The way they glow on the water, with the city lights reflecting across the harbor behind her and even a moon peeking out above — she is just a pretty boat. I may have taken a few too many photos of the exact same shot. Mike did not comment on this, which means he is a very wise man.

What a nice first day back underway. Canada, here we come!
