What a great run today! We left Loggerhead Marina in Vero Beach just before nine and made our way north up the ICW, covering 45 miles and pulling into Cocoa Village Marina by midday. The weather was about as cooperative as you could ask for — light southeast breeze, temperatures climbing into the high 70s by the time we arrived. Not bad at all.

The views along the way did not disappoint. We passed some lovely docks tucked in among the palms, a few little mangrove islands sitting out in the open water like they own the place, and we went under some beautiful high bridges — the kind where you just look up and feel very small and very grateful to be on the water instead of stuck in traffic above. We could see small waterfront communities along the shore and I kept finding myself reaching for the camera. It's that kind of stretch of the ICW.

Once we were tied up and settled, Mike and I headed into Cocoa Village to look around. And that's when things got a little unexpected — in the best possible way. We stumbled onto Willie's Bier Garten, which is exactly what it sounds like. A German beer garden. In Florida. The outdoor courtyard with the picnic tables and string lights was charming, and then we walked inside and I spotted that tap wall. Hofbräu, Schöfferhofer Grapefruit, Tucher, König Ludwig, Weihenstephan, Yuengling, Bitte ein Bit — I mean, where do you even start? I may have taken a photo of the taps before I even sat down, which tells you everything about my priorities.

We ended the evening back aboard the Jackie B III with a very good bottle of Renieri Chianti Classico Gran Selezione — a 2020 that rated 94 points and was made with organic grapes. Honestly a perfect way to close out the day.

Cocoa Village, you were a nice surprise!
