The Jackie B III didn't move an inch today, and honestly that was perfectly fine with us. Our son and his family came to visit us here in Holland, Michigan — which made for a wonderful little reunion right in the middle of our Loop. Rather than staying aboard with us (quarters are cozy enough for two!), they pulled into Holland State Park and set up at Site 72, checking in on August 25th and staying through the 27th.

What made this extra fun is that they stayed in the travel trailer we had bought for Lindsay, before we moved it down to North Carolina. Seeing it parked there in the trees at the campground — the Enlighten sitting so neatly in that wooded site with the awning out — gave me a little smile. It's nice to know it's being put to good use! The campsite was tucked beautifully among tall pines and oaks, with other rigs nearby but still a real sense of being in the woods.

The park looked packed, and I can see why. Holland State Park is a lovely spot. I'm glad they had a comfortable little home base for the weekend.

Days like this are a reminder that the Loop isn't just about the miles covered — sometimes the best part is when your people come to you.

"How good and pleasant it is when God's people live together in unity!"
Psalms 133:1 NIV
