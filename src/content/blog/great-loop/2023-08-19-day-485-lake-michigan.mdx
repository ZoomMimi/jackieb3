We only moved 8.9 nautical miles today, but what a fun little jaunt it was. We left Eldean Shipyard in the early afternoon and made our way into Kalamazoo Lake, which puts you right in the heart of the Saugatuck/Douglas area on Lake Michigan. I'd almost forgotten how pretty this stretch of Michigan shoreline is — those sand dunes rising up behind the trees are really something.

As we came through the inlet you could already tell this was a popular spot. Boats were anchored everywhere along the shore, rafted up and beached right against the sand. People were out enjoying the absolutely perfect summer afternoon — mid-70s, light breeze out of the south. Honestly ideal. We were a little surprised at just how many boats were packed in there, but that's a summer Saturday on Lake Michigan for you.

A couple of the photos I took of all those boats rafted along the shoreline ended up sideways — I'm not sure how I manage to do that every single time, but there you have it. You can get the idea though. It was quite the party atmosphere, with people lined up along the bank on lawn chairs, music going, the works.

After we got settled, someone clearly felt the occasion called for a frozen cocktail. Complete with an orange slice, a lime, and a cherry — practically a fruit salad on the rim of the glass. I will neither confirm nor deny whether there was a second one.

The evening brought a fun surprise at the dock. Right next to us was a Sea Ray out of Saugatuck called *Mystic Rhythm*, and it was hosting what appeared to be a full-on bachelorette party — veils, white dresses, the whole production. They seemed to be having a wonderful time. We wished them well and called it a great first evening in Saugatuck.
