Back in Canada again — and the North Channel does not disappoint on a return visit. We barely moved the boat today, just 0.2 nautical miles in about ten minutes, which might be a new record for the shortest "underway" entry in this entire log. Honestly I think we motored just far enough to shift slips. But what we lacked in miles we more than made up for in activity.

The morning started with what looked like a Looper gathering — one of those wonderful informal seminars where someone sets up a laptop and spreads navigational charts across the table, and the next thing you know the walls are covered in yacht club burgees from all over the continent. There were Great Loop pennants, Canadian flags, and club flags I didn't even recognize — it's like the United Nations of boating in one little room. I sat there with my tote bag and my coffee trying to look like I knew what everyone was talking about. I probably fooled no one.

One of the real highlights was getting a look at *Miss Lily* — what a stunning trawler. That classic blue-and-white hull with the scrollwork lettering is just gorgeous, and even on a grey overcast day she looked like she belonged on a magazine cover. I had to get my picture taken next to her. She reminded me why we fell in love with this whole lifestyle in the first place.

After all that, we laced up our hiking shoes and headed into the Canadian forest for a trail walk. The woods here in the North Channel area are something else — big pines, mossy rocks, a little stream here and there, fallen logs that look like they've been there since before anyone was keeping track. The trail was rocky enough to keep things interesting, and I will not admit how many times I grabbed someone's arm for balance. We made it to a lovely overlook above the water where the pines frame the view perfectly, and we stood there grinning like we'd just summited something much more impressive than we actually had. The photos tell a happier story than my legs did afterward.

It is so good to be back up here. The North Channel earns every bit of its reputation.
