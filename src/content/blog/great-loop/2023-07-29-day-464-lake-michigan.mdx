We are working our way back toward Canadian waters for another taste of the North Channel — a return we have been looking forward to ever since we fell in love with that part of the world during our 2022 Georgian Bay leg. But before we headed back out on the water, we had a wonderful surprise right here in Montague, Michigan.

We wandered into town and discovered the streets were blocked off for what turned out to be a classic car cruise night. What a treat! The crowds were out, the evening sky was beautiful, and the cars — oh my goodness, the cars. Every direction you turned there was something jaw-dropping.

There were Corvettes and Mustangs, a gorgeous deep-green early Model A sedan with whitewall tires, a cherry-red Chevelle SS convertible with the hood up showing off that engine, a red-and-white '55 Chevy Bel Air, and a stunning red Thunderbird that looked like it just rolled off a showroom floor. One of my favorites was a big burgundy Buick convertible being driven by two ladies who looked like they were having the absolute best night of their lives — and honestly, same.

There was also a military-style Jeep with "NOT FORGOTTEN" painted on the side flying American flags — that one stopped me in my tracks. Very moving.

And then there was *Babydoll* — a custom raw aluminum hot rod that looked like something out of a science fiction movie. Nobody could quite figure out what to call it, which I think was exactly the point. It was spectacular.

One of the more historically impressive cars was a sleek black pre-war sedan with whitewall tires and wire wheels — the kind of car that makes you think of old movies and country estates. There was also a lovely powder-blue Studebaker that I could have stared at all evening.

The whole downtown was just buzzing — street closed, people everywhere, music in the air. We felt lucky to have stumbled right into the middle of it. Sometimes the Loop surprises you on land just as much as it does on the water.

Oh — and back on the boat I noticed someone had a copy of *Rum Curious* by Fred Minnick sitting out. Apparently there is reading material appropriate to a boating life after all. 😄
