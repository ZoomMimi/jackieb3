What a fun day on the water, even if it wasn't exactly the longest run we've ever made! We're still based at Eldean Shipyard up in Macatawa, getting the Jackie B III ready for our return trip to Canada. Yes, Canada — we loved it so much during the Loop that we just had to go back. More on that soon!

This morning we picked up three lovely ladies and took them out for a little cruise over to Anchorage Marina and back. The water on Lake Macatawa was that gorgeous blue-green color, and you could see the waterfront homes lining the shore behind us as we motored along. It was one of those mornings where you just feel grateful to be out on the water. Our guests looked perfectly at home settled into the cockpit seating, soaking it all in — sunglasses on, smiles ready. I love sharing this boat life with people who appreciate it.

The cruise itself was just shy of a half hour each way, but honestly the company made it feel like much more. We were back at Eldean by 12:34, so the whole outing clocked in at a little over three hours underway total — short and sweet.

Of course, no day on the Jackie B III is ever entirely carefree. Mike spent a good chunk of time with the engine hatch open, and I dutifully photographed every inch of those Volvo Penta engines so we'd have a record of exactly what we're dealing with before heading north. I don't pretend to know what I'm looking at down there — fuel injectors, injector lines, all sorts of bolts and connections — but Mike does, and that's what counts. We even have a fuel line tagged with a little piece of green masking tape and a handwritten note, which I'm choosing to interpret as "organized" rather than "duct-tape engineering." I'll let Mike weigh in on that one.

Canada is calling. We just want to make sure the engines are ready to answer.
