What a treat this day turned out to be! We'll be heading north to Canada soon, but before we left Holland we got to do something really special — take my mom Sally, our beloved Tutu, out for a cruise on Lake Macatawa here in Holland, Michigan. She brought along two of her friends, and the three of them settled right into the cockpit seating like they owned the place. I have to say, they looked completely at home out there!

We ran from Eldean Shipyard over to Boatwerks and back, which was just the right amount of adventure for a Monday morning. The water on Lake Macatawa was beautiful — that gorgeous deep blue-green color it gets on a calm day — and the homes along the shoreline looked absolutely stunning. The temperature was mild, barely into the 60s, so everyone had their jackets on, but the sun was out and the company more than made up for any chill in the air.

Watching Tutu out on the water with Ann and Kay, laughing and taking it all in, was honestly one of those moments I want to hold onto forever. Mike did a wonderful job as captain for these three lovely ladies, and I think they thoroughly approved of the ride. We covered about 16 and a half nautical miles in just over three hours — not a bad morning's work at all.

Days like this are exactly why we do this.
