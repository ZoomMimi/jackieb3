What a day on the water — and what a finish to it!

We left Myrtle Beach this morning around 10:45 with perfect conditions, mild temps and just a gentle breeze out of the WSW. The ICW through here is really something. Wide open stretches of marsh grass and sandbars, little wooded islands, storm clouds building on the horizon that thankfully stayed just far enough away. I kept sneaking back to the stern to watch the wake rolling out behind us through all of it. 38.6 nautical miles and about five hours of some genuinely beautiful North Carolina coastal scenery.

The House of Blues wristband on Mike's wrist is apparently now a permanent fixture. His Garmin says he's in recovery mode — I think the watch and I are in agreement on that one.

But the real highlight of this stop was Bob Creech. Bob is a legendary AGLCA Harbor Host here in Southport, and he does something that I think is just about the nicest thing you can do for a tired looper: he invites you to his porch for docktails. And that porch! Right there looking out over the water, live oaks framing the view, a shrimp boat working its way past in the channel — it doesn't get much better than that. We gathered with a wonderful group of fellow loopers and Bob's other guests, settled into the rocking chairs, and just exhaled.

Morningstar Marinas has us squared away for the night — dock staff Colby had everything sorted before we even tied up. The marina is well laid out and easy to navigate, which after a day of bridges — and we counted quite a few between here and Myrtle Beach — is exactly what you want.

Now if you'll excuse me, I need to decide between the Nauti Negroni and the Edgewater Ke-Ke. Tough choices on the Loop.
