What a day in Savannah! We moved the Jackie B III just 17.6 nautical miles — a short hop down the ICW from Thunderbolt Marine over to Harbor Town Yacht Basin. It was a beautiful afternoon run, warm and sunny, and we were tied up and settled in by early afternoon. Harbor Town is such a great spot — that little lighthouse right there on the waterfront is pretty hard to resist as a selfie backdrop, and well, we are not known for our restraint in that department.

With the boat tucked in and the engine manuals spread out on the table — yes, we spent a good chunk of the afternoon with our noses in the Cummins generator specs, tracking down part numbers for the heat exchanger. Glamorous, I know. Boating life! — we figured we'd earned a proper evening out.

And Savannah at night does not disappoint. We found ourselves on what the sign called the "Historic Steps" — apparently for use by people who actually pay attention to signs, which we were only marginally doing — laughing at ourselves and snapping photos while the streets glowed with string lights below. Mike had his best wide-eyed look going for the camera. It's a good one. I may have been equally ridiculous. The city was lively and gorgeous and we just soaked it all in.

There is something about Savannah after dark that feels a little magic. We are so lucky to be doing this.

"He has made everything beautiful in its time."
