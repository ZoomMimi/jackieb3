I have to be honest — I look at these photos and I'm a little embarrassed that I didn't take more of them during the daylight hours. But sometimes a day just gets away from you in the best possible way, and before you know it the sun is going down and the moon is coming up and you think, *well, this is pretty good right here.*

The moon was just about full, and it rose over the river in the most spectacular way. I kept trying to capture it and I'm not sure any of my photos do it justice — you really just had to be standing there on that grassy bank with the water so still it mirrored the whole sky back at you. A towboat pushing a long string of barges came by at just the right moment and the lights reflected off the water alongside the moon's glow. I took about a dozen shots trying to get it right. You can see for yourself how that went.

Mike, of course, was not going to let an evening like this pass without a proper campfire. There was a big old fire ring on the grounds and he got right to work on it — grinning like he'd been waiting all day for the chance, which honestly he probably had. The flames got going really well and I have to say he looked very pleased with himself poking at it with that long stick. I am married to a man who considers a roaring fire a personal accomplishment. I've made my peace with this.

We walked down to the river's edge a few times as it got darker, just to watch the moon climb higher and the barges drift past. It was one of those evenings where you don't really need to say much. We took a couple of selfies — two happy people standing by a beautiful river.

Oh, and we had a little visitor during the day. The tiniest rabbit you have ever seen, tucked right into the grass and brush near the bank. It sat there so still I almost missed it. Sweet little thing.

It was a good day to just *be* somewhere.
