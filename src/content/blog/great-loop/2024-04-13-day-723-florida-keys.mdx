What a day. We had family visiting and the weather was absolutely gorgeous — not a cloud in that deep Florida Keys blue sky — so we did what any sensible boat family does: we put everybody in something that floats and pushed off.

My husband was on the paddle board looking completely at ease, the way he always does out there. Meanwhile the kids — every size, every age it seemed — were loaded into kayaks. The little ones had their life jackets snapped tight and their paddles in hand, and they were *serious* about it. One of the smallest paddlers had the whole yellow kayak to himself and looked like he was captaining a ship. I loved every second of watching them weave through the marina canal, past the boats tied up along the docks. You could see a couple of the other boaters stopping to watch the little flotilla go by — I don't blame them, it was quite the parade.

The adults managed the herding pretty impressively too, I have to say. One mom had a toddler tucked right into the front of her green Heritage kayak, paddling away with a Bending Branches paddle like it was the most natural thing in the world. Hat on, game face on. I was taking pictures from the dock and cheering them on — someone had to stay dry long enough to document this.

Once everyone had their sea legs, the whole group paddled out beyond the marina into more open water. The kids were hollering back and forth between boats, which I'm sure the other marina guests appreciated. 😄

And then — best part — we found ourselves a little white sand beach tucked back in the mangroves. The kids tumbled out and ran around in the sand while the grown-ups caught their breath. Somebody had packed snacks, which made everyone very popular. It was one of those moments where you look around and think: *this* is what it's all about.
