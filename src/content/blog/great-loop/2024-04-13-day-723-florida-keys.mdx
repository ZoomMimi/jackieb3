What a day! Our daughter came down with her two kids — the four-year-old and the seven-year-old — and we were not going to let a single minute go to waste. Mike got the paddle board in the water, our daughter loaded up the green kayak with her littlest tucked in front, and the older one got the blue kayak all to herself. Watching that girl paddle her own boat around the marina channel like she'd been doing it for years — I just couldn't stop smiling.

The whole flotilla launched right out from the marina, weaving past the docks and working their way through the channel. Mike on the board, the kids in their little kayaks, everyone doing their own thing. The yellow kayak made a few appearances too — that tiny one in the orange life jacket was determined. I watched from the Jackie B III and honestly, it was one of those moments where you feel like the luckiest person on the planet.

After the paddling we made it out to a little sandy stretch tucked in among the mangroves — white sand, blue sky, kids running every direction. It's the kind of spot that's only really accessible by water, which somehow makes it feel like your own private piece of the Keys. They snacked, they explored, they wore each other out. Perfect.

These are the days the Loop is really for.
