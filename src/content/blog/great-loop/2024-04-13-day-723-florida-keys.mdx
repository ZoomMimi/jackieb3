What a day on the water! We got the whole fleet out — kayaks in green, blue, and yellow, Mike on the SUP board, and little ones tucked in with their life jackets and paddles, doing their very best to look like they knew exactly what they were doing. (Some of them actually did!)

We launched right from the marina, weaving through the channel past the other boats. I loved watching the kids figure out that a paddle works better when you actually put it in the water. There was a lot of spinning in circles at first. A lot.

Mike looked completely at home on his board as usual, towing the little yellow kayak behind him at one point while its tiny captain just grinned and went along for the ride. That little guy in the orange life jacket had absolutely no complaints about that arrangement.

Once we got out of the channel and onto the open water, we found a beautiful little sandy spot tucked into the mangroves. The kids tumbled out and ran around on the white sand while the grown-ups caught their breath. Snacks were distributed. Everyone was happy. The kind of afternoon that reminds you exactly why we're doing this whole Loop in the first place.
