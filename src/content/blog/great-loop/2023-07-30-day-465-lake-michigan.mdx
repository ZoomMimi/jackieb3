We are back in Canada — or rather, we *were* back in Canada for a glorious stretch through the North Channel — but today's story actually finds us on Lake Michigan, making our way south and homeward after that wonderful return to Ontario waters. Mike had us underway just after six in the morning, which meant I was still very much in the "coffee is non-negotiable" phase of my day. I'm not sure I said a complete sentence until at least the second cup.

The reward for the early alarm, though, was absolutely worth it. The sunrise over Lake Michigan was something else entirely — all deep orange and pink along the horizon, with dramatic clouds rolling overhead and the wake stretching out behind us like a ribbon on the water. I just sat there in the salon with my mug and let it happen. Some mornings you just have to stop trying to do anything and simply look.

Mike, meanwhile, was keeping a close eye on the Garmin radar. There was a solid line of weather showing up to the west — you can see it in the photo, that bright red band — so he was tracking it carefully while I was busy being mesmerized by the sunrise. I appreciate that one of us has our priorities in order at all times. (It is not always me.)

We covered 43 nautical miles in just over four hours and pulled into Eldean Shipyard at Holland Harbor right around 9:30 in the morning. Not a bad way to spend a Sunday.
