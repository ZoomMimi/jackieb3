We said goodbye to Harbor Town Yacht Basin on Hilton Head this morning and pushed off just after 10. The weather was warm and the wind was light out of the southwest — really a lovely morning to be on the water. We made a quick stop along the way, then continued up through the ICW, passing under the J.E. McTeer Bridge and the Woods Memorial Bridge before pulling into Lady's Island Marina in Beaufort, SC by mid-afternoon.

I have to say, tying up with that view of the red and white lighthouse right behind the Jackie B III might be one of my favorite dock photos of the whole Loop. It just doesn't get more picturesque than that.

Before we left Hilton Head, we had managed to squeeze in a beach ride — yes, we actually got the bikes out! There may or may not be photographic evidence of me in bike shorts that I will not be commenting on further. The sand there is so impossibly white and the water that pale greenish-gray that makes you want to just stand there and stare. We were happy to do exactly that for a little while.

Once we were settled in at Lady's Island Marina, we unzipped the menu at Lady's Island — and wow, did they take their seafood seriously. Low Country Boil, she-crab soup, shrimp and grits, oysters Rockefeller… I could have ordered half the menu. Beaufort knows how to eat.

Heading up through the waterway we also passed what the water tower clearly identified as a Marines installation — always a good reminder of the proud military presence all along this coast.
