Well, today did not go quite according to plan — but honestly, some of the best days don't.

We ended up in a parking lot situation involving a red North Carolina pickup truck (plate JFP-5558, "Squad!" decal on the rear window — I have the photos to prove it) and our vehicle. Nothing dramatic, just the kind of slow-speed parking lot encounter that makes your stomach drop. We got everything sorted out with the young man involved, who was perfectly pleasant about the whole thing. Still. Not exactly how I envisioned the afternoon going.

What turned the day around completely was stumbling into a gallery called **Warrior Poet**. I honestly wasn't sure what to expect from the name, but once we were inside I couldn't stop looking. The walls were lined with this remarkable series of framed prints — numbered one through five — each one exploring the human heart in a different way. Literally and figuratively. Battles, chains, angels, a man kneeling before what looked like a divine figure, a broken man being embraced in light. Heavy stuff, but beautifully done. The kind of art that stops you mid-step and makes you think about what's actually going on inside a person. I stood in front of #5 — the embrace — for a good long while.

A harp pierced by an arrow hung under the "Warrior Poet" sign. Somehow that said it all.

"Create in me a pure heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me."
Psalms 51:10 NIV
