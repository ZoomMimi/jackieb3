## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Trusting a single automated signal for date/location correction | Repeated false positives this session: a nonsensical cross-country "Trent-Severn Waterway to Stock Island FL" origin (nearest "confident" match was 18 months away), a state-level geocode match that passed despite the real place being 100mi off, a waterway-feature name ("Chesapeake Bay") forward-geocoded to an arbitrary point and flagged as "wrong", a null-GPS photo averaging into a (0,0) centroid. | advisory | Always cross-check at least two independent signals before proposing a fix: real photo GPS/timestamp, Nebo map VISUAL content (view the image, don't just trust OCR'd stats), post narrative text, and now also original Blogger publish date — none alone is authoritative. |
| Blog publish date ≠ event date | The original Blogger post for "Day 23 Solomons Island" is published 2022-05-14, but the real event (confirmed by Nebo map content) happened 2022-05-13 — Barbara posted a day late. Assumed publish date was ground truth and nearly caused a wrong rename before the map image was actually viewed. | blocking | Never rename/redate a post from Blogger publish date alone. Always view the actual Nebo map image content (start/end points, place labels) and compare to the post's own narrative before deciding the real event date. |

<decisions_made>

- Nebo map visual content (viewing the actual image, reading place labels/start-end points directly) is the most reliable single signal — more reliable than OCR'd distance stats (which have frequent parsing failures, e.g. `maxSpeedKts: 77.1` nonsense values) and more reliable than any date field (frontmatter, Nebo's self-reported date, or Blogger publish date — all three can independently be off by a day or more).
- Sequential, human-confirmed walkthrough (starting from post 1) was chosen over continued automation after multiple real false positives from automated classification — this is intentionally slower but more trustworthy. Do not revert to batch-automation without the user's explicit direction.
- "Day N" / "Days N-M" labels in titles are never recomputed from calendar arithmetic — preserved verbatim from the original blogger's own count.
- Gallery-array photo reordering (Phase 6 posts only) is safe to automate; inline-image reordering in legacy post prose is not.
</decisions_made>
